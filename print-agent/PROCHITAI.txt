PIZZA ROOM - pechat zakazov na kuhnyu

1. Raspakuyte etu papku v lyuboe mesto (naprimer, Rabochiy stol).
2. V adminke: Nastroyki -> "Pechat na kuhnyu" -> "Sozdat klyuch" -> Kopirovat.
3. Zapustite USTANOVIT.bat (esli Windows sprosit - "Podrobnee" -> "Vypolnit v lyubom sluchae").
4. Vstavte klyuch (pravaya knopka myshi), vyberite nomer printera.
5. Vyydet probnyy blank - gotovo. Programma zapuskaetsya sama pri vklyuchenii kompyutera.

Otklyuchit: UDALIT.bat
Zhurnal raboty: %LOCALAPPDATA%\PizzaRoomPrint\log.txt
